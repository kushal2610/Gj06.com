How We Make It

From flour to fresh-baked, every day starts before sunrise. Here's the journey of our pav.

Step 1

Mix

Flour, yeast & water blended into a smooth dough.

Step 2

Knead & Rise

Rested & proved until soft and airy.

Step 3

Shape

Rolled & portioned into pav by hand.

Step 4

Bake

Baked golden & fresh in small batches.

Step 5

Deliver

Packed & delivered to your door, daily.
